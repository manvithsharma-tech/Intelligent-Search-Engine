Comparative efficacy of topical garlic extract and 20% podophyllin solution in the treatment of female genital warts: a triple-blind randomized clinical trial.

Genital warts caused by human papillomavirus are common sexually transmitted infections, with no single therapy providing consistent clearance. Garlic extract has antiviral and antiproliferative properties and may offer a low-cost treatment. To compare the efficacy and safety of topical 5% garlic extract with 20% podophyllin solution in women with genital warts. Seventy-four women were randomized to 5% garlic extract ( Mean wart size decreased from 1.61 ± 0.81 to 0.60 ± 0.82 mm with garlic extract and from 2.96 ± 7.99 to 0.62 ± 0.73 mm with podophyllin. The between-group Both treatments reduced wart size and count, with similar response rates. Four-week follow-up precluded assessment of durability and recurrence.
