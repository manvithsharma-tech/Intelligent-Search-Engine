Phase 1 Study of Anito-cel, a d-Domain BCMA CAR T Cell for Refractory or Recurrent Myeloma.

Anitocabtagene autoleucel (anito-cel), a B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA)-directed autologous chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy with a synthetic d-domain binder (ddBCMA), may have efficacy in patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma. All 38 patients had an adverse event during the treatment period, and 37 patients (97%) had an adverse event of grade 3 or higher.
