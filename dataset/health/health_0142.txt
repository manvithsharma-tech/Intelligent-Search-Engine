Immunogenicity, safety, and efficacy of rurioctocog alfa pegol in previously untreated patients with severe hemophilia A.

In severe hemophilia A, factor VIII (FVIII) replacement is essential, but FVIII inhibitor development can be serious. This phase 3, prospective, uncontrolled, open-label, multicenter study (NCT02615691) investigated the immunogenicity, efficacy, and safety of rurioctocog alfa pegol for severe hemophilia A in previously untreated patients. Patients received intravenous prophylaxis (25-50 IU/kg, up to 80 IU/kg, ≥1 dose weekly) and/or on-demand therapy (10-50 IU/kg, up to 80 IU/kg depending on bleed severity). The primary endpoint was FVIII inhibitor incidence (≥0.6 BU/mL [Nijmegen modification of Bethesda assay]) in the full analysis set (patients with ≥100 exposure-days [EDs] or who developed an inhibitor). We present the final data. Of 146 enrolled patients, 120 received ≥1 dose; all were male, and 55.8% had a family history of hemophilia A. Median (range) age was 9.6 (1.2-60.0) months.
