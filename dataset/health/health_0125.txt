Blood-based biomarkers in patients with recurrent, persistent, or metastatic endometrial cancer treated with olaparib and cediranib: Results from NRG-GY012.

The NRG-GY012 phase 2 trial evaluated olaparib, cediranib, and the combination in 120 patients with recurrent, persistent, or metastatic endometrial cancer (EC). Predictive and prognostic associations between blood-based biomarkers and clinical outcomes were assessed. Plasma samples collected at baseline, cycle 4 day 1, and disease progression were analyzed for circulating protein biomarkers (Angiome). The primary analysis evaluated the predictive impact of IL-6 on progression-free survival (PFS) and overall survival (OS). The predictive and prognostic impact of all other markers on PFS and OS were also evaluated. Ninety-six baseline plasma samples were included in this analysis. Results from GOG-218 identified IL-6 was predictive of clinical benefit using a threshold of 2.9 pg/mL. Although the 2.9 pg/mL cut point did not validate in GY012, IL-6 associated with OS using the median level of 4.2 pg/mL as the cut point.
