Giredestrant plus Everolimus in Advanced Breast Cancer.

Giredestrant and everolimus target the estrogen receptor (ER) pathway and the phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K)-protein kinase B (AKT)-mammalian target of rapamycin (mTOR) pathway, respectively, which are implicated in endocrine-therapy resistance. In this phase 3, open-label, randomized trial, we enrolled patients with ER-positive, human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2)-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer who had disease progression or recurrence after receipt of a cyclin-dependent kinase 4 and 6 (CDK4/6) inhibitor plus endocrine therapy. Patients were assigned, in a 1:1 ratio, to receive giredestrant plus everolimus (each given orally) or standard endocrine therapy (i.e., exemestane, fulvestrant, or tamoxifen) plus everolimus.
