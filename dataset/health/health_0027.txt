Impact of Diabetes on Outcomes of Contemporary PCI Guided by OCT vs Angiography: The ILUMIEN IV Trial.

Patients with diabetes are at higher risk for adverse events after percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) compared with patients without diabetes. This study sought to assess the influence of diabetes and complex lesions on the outcomes of patients undergoing PCI with and without optical coherence tomography (OCT) guidance during a follow-up period of 2 years. Patients in ILUMIEN IV randomized to OCT-guided vs angiography-guided PCI were grouped into those with (n = 1,044) and without diabetes (n = 1,443). Study endpoints were target vessel failure (TVF) and serious major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE). Patients with diabetes and complex lesions were at particularly high risk for adverse outcomes after PCI.
