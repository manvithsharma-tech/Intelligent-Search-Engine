Isosorbide Mononitrate/Cilostazol for Lacunar Cerebral Small Vessel Disease-Outcomes at 6 Months in the LACI-2 Randomized Controlled Trial.

Lacunar stroke can cause cognitive decline and dependency. The LACI-2 (Lacunar Intervention Trial-2) trial showed that 12 months of treatment with isosorbide-mononitrate (ISMN) or cilostazol improved these outcomes. We tested whether this effect was present at 6 months. LACI-2 was a prospective randomized open-label blinded-end point 2×2-factorial phase-2b trial assessing feasibility, safety, and proof-of-concept of 1 year of ISMN (40-60 mg) or cilostazol (200 mg). Participants aged >30 years had clinical lacunar stroke, compatible neuroimaging, and capacity to consent. At 6 months, participants allocated to ISMN versus control had fewer composite events (adjusted odds ratio [OR], 0.74 [95% CI, 0.55-0.99]) and improved stroke impact (Mann-Whitney difference, -0.15 [95% CI -0.25 to -0.05]).
